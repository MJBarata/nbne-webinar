Audrey signed up for one charity race at 42. It turned into a second career. She's a Level 3 British Triathlon coach and a World Triathlon Continental Technical Official, and this year she was Head Referee for the Women's Elite race at WTCS London. In 2020 the BBC named her Unsung Hero for the London region.
