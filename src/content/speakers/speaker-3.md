Audrey came to triathlon at 42 to raise money for charity, and one race became
a second career. A Level 3 British Triathlon Coach and World Triathlon
Continental Technical Official, she was Head Referee for the Women's Elite WTCS
in London in 2026 and has advised British Triathlon, Sport England and UK Sport
on diversity and representation. She was named BBC Sports Personality of the
Year Unsung Hero for London in 2020.
