Eleanor leads Triathlon Ireland's national initiatives to increase female
engagement and build inclusive environments at every level of the sport, from
strategy and programme design to advocacy for women as athletes, coaches,
officials and leaders. Recognised for her work in community and grassroots
sport, she also volunteers with The Hygiene Hub, Special Olympics Ireland and
her local triathlon club.
