Eleanor leads Triathlon Ireland's work on getting more women into the sport and keeping them there, as athletes, coaches, officials and leaders. She writes the strategy and then goes out and runs the programmes. Outside work she volunteers with The Hygiene Hub, Special Olympics Ireland and her local club.
