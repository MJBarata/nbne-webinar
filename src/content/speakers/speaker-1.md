Debbie is a clinical psychologist who has led Triathlon South Africa since 2009 and is now 1st Vice President of World Triathlon. She helped bring the first ITU Paratriathlon competition and a WTS event to Africa. In 2012 she was named South Africa's Most Influential Woman in Business and Government in the sport category.
